The small screens that never make it into a case study.

Components, motion studies, empty states and the interface details I care about more than I can justify in a project write-up.

Why a page for this

Case studies are about judgement. They're the right format for explaining why a product went one way instead of another, and they're a bad format for showing whether you can actually draw.

This page is for the second thing. It's the interface work at close range — the states, the spacing, the transitions, the four versions of an error message before one of them was kind enough to ship.
